# Cluster A.V - Constitutional Principles of the Kernel

- [A.7 - Strict Distinction (Clarity Lattice) (A.7)](01-a7-a7-strict-distinction-clarity-lattice.md) — Prevents four recurring mix-ups: role vs. behaviour, description vs. actual work, actors vs. documents, and the thing under discussion vs. the text describing it.
- [A.8 - Universal Core Principle (A.8)](02-a8-a8-universal-core-principle.md) — Ensures that a new core concept is genuinely universal before admitting it, by requiring it to hold across at least three fundamentally different fields.
- [A.9 - Cross‑Scale Consistency (C‑3) (A.9)](03-a9-a9-crossscale-consistency-c3.md) — Prevents the logic used at a part level from contradicting the logic used at the whole level, so portfolio roll-ups and unit-level tests tell the same story.
- [A.10 - Evidence Graph Referring: Claim-Bound Evidence and Provenance Graph (A.10)](04-a10-a10-evidence-graph-referring-claim-bound-evidence-and-proven.md) — Stops dashboards, credentials, and generated explanations from acting as evidence by requiring every claim to name its actual carrier, producer, time window, and admissible use.
- [A.11 - Ontological Parsimony (A.11)](05-a11-a11-ontological-parsimony.md) — Keeps the concept vocabulary lean by requiring that a new concept be added only when existing ones genuinely cannot express the claim without material loss.
- [A.12 - Acting-Side Externalization and Reflexive Split (A.12)](06-a12-a12-acting-side-externalization-and-reflexive-split.md) — Repairs "the system fixed itself" language by separating the thing being changed from the actor doing the changing, so accountability never disappears.
- [A.13 - The Agential Role & Agency Spectrum (A.13)](07-a13-a13-the-agential-role-agency-spectrum.md) — Gives teams a measurable, gradable way to describe how autonomous a system is so "agent" stops being a vague label and becomes an auditable, evidence-backed claim.
- [A.14 - Advanced Mereology: Components, Portions, Aspects & Phases (A.14)](08-a14-a14-advanced-mereology-components-portions-aspects-phases.md) — Disambiguates "part of" claims by distinguishing physical components, measured portions, aspects, and time phases so composition and responsibility claims stay sound.
- [A.15 - Role–Method–Work Alignment (Contextual Enactment) (A.15)](09-a15-a15-rolemethodwork-alignment-contextual-enactment.md) — Separates who is responsible, how the work is supposed to be done, what the plan says, and what actually happened so teams stop arguing over a single "process" cue.
- [A.15.1 - U.Work (A.15.1)](10-a151-a151-uwork.md) — Governs a performed, dated work occurrence so teams cannot count a plan, a method description, or a dashboard as if work already happened.
- [A.15.2 - U.WorkPlan (A.15.2)](11-a152-a152-uworkplan.md) — Governs an intended-work record so schedules and tickets are not mistaken for performed work, evidence, or gate approval.
- [A.15.3 - SlotFillingsPlanItem (A.15.3)](12-a153-a153-slotfillingsplanitem.md) — Lets a work plan pin the exact references and policy choices needed before work starts so the baseline is reproducible and variance against it is visible.
- [A.15.4 - Work-Relevant Appearance-Based Reliance Repair (A.15.4)](13-a154-a154-work-relevant-appearance-based-reliance-repair.md) — Stops a team from acting on something that looks approved or ready by requiring the actual governing source to be named before the work or reliance claim proceeds.
- [A.15.5 - Work-Entry Readiness and Full-Kit Preparation (A.15.5)](14-a155-a155-work-entry-readiness-and-full-kit-preparation.md) — Lets a team check whether inputs, resources, and gate conditions are truly ready before committing to start work, without treating "ready" as a gate decision or work occurrence.
- [A.16 - Language-State Move Coordination (A.16)](15-a16-a16-language-state-move-coordination.md) — Coordinates the next admissible move when a team has a cue but not yet a claim, so early signals are not prematurely forced into requirements or work records.
- [A.16.0 - U.LanguageStateMoveTrajectory - Optional trajectory-account normal form over the language-state U.CharacteristicSpace (A.16.0)](16-a160-a160-ulanguagestatemovetrajectory-optional-trajectory-accoun.md) — Lets a team publish the history of how a statement evolved through drafts, forks, and handoffs when that lineage itself has governance value.
- [A.16.1 - U.PreArticulationCuePack (A.16.1)](17-a161-a161-uprearticulationcuepack.md) — Preserves an early signal or hunch in a visible form before it is ready to become a claim, so it is not lost or prematurely promoted into a route decision.
- [A.16.2 - Reopen / SketchBackoff / Respecify (A.16.2)](18-a162-a162-reopen-sketchbackoff-respecify.md) — Gives a team a first-class, auditable way to back out of an over-committed framing, reopen a question, or retire a branch without hiding that the retreat happened.
- [A.17 - Canonical “Characteristic” (A.CHR‑NORM) (A.17)](19-a17-a17-canonical-characteristic-achrnorm.md) — Fixes a single word for "the aspect being measured" so teams stop conflating what is measured with how it is expressed, preventing unsafe arithmetic across incompatible scales.
- [A.18 - Minimal CSLC in Kernel (Characteristic ⟷ Scale ⟷ Level ⟷ Coordinate) (A.CSLC‑KERNEL) (A.18)](20-a18-a18-minimal-cslc-in-kernel-characteristic-scale-level-coordi.md) — Provides the minimal four-part vocabulary (what is measured, on which scale, as which level, at which coordinate) so any measurement can be interpreted and compared without ambiguity.
- [A.19 - CharacteristicSpace & Dynamics Hook (A.CHR‑SPACE) (A.19)](21-a19-a19-characteristicspace-dynamics-hook-achrspace.md) — Lets a team declare the full set of aspects and scales being tracked so comparability boundaries, thresholds, and dynamics are inspectable rather than hidden inside dashboards or scores.
- [A.19.ECS - Evaluation CharacteristicSpace Construction (A.19.ECS)](22-a19ecs-a19ecs-evaluation-characteristicspace-construction.md) — Helps a team build the evaluation criteria before starting an improvement loop, so "better" has a defined meaning instead of being chosen after the fact.
- [A.19.SPR - State-Family Precision Restoration (A.19.SPR)](23-a19spr-a19spr-state-family-precision-restoration.md) — Repairs vague state words like "current", "ready", "accepted", or "stable" before they are mistaken for evidence, gate passage, or work completion.
- [A.19.SOURCE-SET-SPACE-SUBSTRATE - Source-Set and Search/Outcome-Space Substrate (A.19.SOURCE)](24-a19source-a19source-set-space-substrate-source-set-and-searchoutcome-s.md) — Makes the link between what a team searched, what came out, and where distortion or uncertainty lives explicit so the source-to-outcome relation can be inspected and replayed.
- [A.19.DECLARED-SUBSTRATE-INTERPRETIVE-VIEW - Declared-Substrate Interpretive View (A.19.DECLARED)](25-a19declared-a19declared-substrate-interpretive-view-declared-substrate-i.md) — Lets a team read an already-declared source-outcome substrate through a thinner or fuller interpretive lens without changing the underlying data or silently redefining it.
- [A.19.CN - CN‑frame (comparability & normalization) (A.19.CN)](26-a19cn-a19cn-cnframe-comparability-normalization.md) — Governs the rules and audit card for making measurements comparable across contexts so normalization choices are pinned and traceable rather than hidden inside aggregation steps.
- [A.19.CHR - CHRMechanismSuite (A.19.CHR)](27-a19chr-a19chr-chrmechanismsuite.md) — Describes the full characterization toolkit as a named, coordinated suite so teams can plan, pin, and carry through the normalize-score-compare-select pipeline as a reproducible baseline.
- [A.19.UNM - Unified Normalization Mechanism (UNM) (A.19.UNM)](28-a19unm-a19unm-unified-normalization-mechanism-unm.md) — Governs how raw measured values are re-expressed on a common scale under declared rules so comparison steps know exactly what equivalence they are relying on.
- [A.19.UINDM - Unified Indicatorization Mechanism (UINDM) (A.19.UINDM)](29-a19uindm-a19uindm-unified-indicatorization-mechanism-uindm.md) — Governs the explicit selection of which measured aspects to carry forward into scoring so the choice of indicators is policy-driven and auditable, not arbitrary.
- [A.19.USCM - Unified Scoring Mechanism, USCM (A.19.USCM)](30-a19uscm-a19uscm-unified-scoring-mechanism-uscm.md) — Governs how admitted measurement profiles are turned into scores under declared methods and evidence rules so a score cannot be produced without a traceable, auditable basis.
- [A.19.ULSAM - Unified Lawful Scale Aggregation Mechanism (ULSAM) (A.19.ULSAM)](31-a19ulsam-a19ulsam-unified-lawful-scale-aggregation-mechanism-ulsam.md) — Governs how individual scores are legally rolled up into a total so aggregation always respects scale type and does not silently average things that cannot be averaged.
- [A.19.CPM - Unified Comparison Mechanism (CPM) (A.19.CPM)](32-a19cpm-a19cpm-unified-comparison-mechanism-cpm.md) — Governs pairwise comparison under a named comparator so the result preserves ties and incomparability rather than forcing a hidden winner.
- [A.19.SelectorMechanism - Unified Selection Kernel, SelectorMechanism (A.19.S)](33-a19s-a19selectormechanism-unified-selection-kernel-selectormechan.md) — Governs how a team picks from admitted candidates under explicit criteria so selection never hides a threshold, forces a single winner, or relies on an undeclared scoring shortcut.
- [A.20 - Flow Constraint Validity — Eulerian (A.20)](34-a20-a20-flow-constraint-validity-eulerian.md) — Checks whether a single transformation step satisfies its own declared constraints before any pass/fail gate decision is made, keeping step validity and gate authority separate.
- [A.21 — GateProfilization: OperationalGate(profile) (GateFit core) (A.21)](35-a21-a21-gateprofilization-operationalgateprofile-gatefit-core.md) — Governs a formal gate decision that aggregates declared checks into a traceable pass/degrade/block result so a green tile or readiness badge is never mistaken for gate passage.
- [A.22 - Structure and Structural Views (STRUCT-CAL) (A.22)](36-a22-a22-structure-and-structural-views-struct-cal.md) — Helps a team choose and describe the specific structural relation, constraint, or arrangement that matters for the next engineering decision, without treating "structure" as a catch-all.
- [A.22.CGUS - Constraint-Governed Unfolding Structure (A.22.CGUS)](37-a22cgus-a22cgus-constraint-governed-unfolding-structure.md) — How to treat a diagram or step list as the rules for what can come next, not a fixed sequence everyone must follow.
