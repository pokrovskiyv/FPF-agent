# Part B — Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster

- [B.1 - Holon Aggregation and Part-Whole Construction (B.1)](01-b1-b1-holon-aggregation-and-part-whole-construction.md) — Helps teams say precisely how several parts make up a whole without mixing up membership, roles, or methods.
- [B.1.1 - Dependency Structure and Relation Grounding (B.1.1)](02-b11-b11-dependency-structure-and-relation-grounding.md) — Helps teams untangle what a dependency diagram actually claims before using it to justify a whole-level conclusion.
- [B.1.2 - System Aggregation and Holon Delimitation (B.1.2)](03-b12-b12-system-aggregation-and-holon-delimitation.md) — Helps teams define where a physical or operational system ends and its environment begins when rolling up system-level claims.
- [B.1.3 - Γ_epist - Knowledge‑Specific Aggregation (B.1.3)](04-b13-b13-_epist-knowledgespecific-aggregation.md) — Helps teams combine claims, models, and datasets without inflating confidence or erasing where the evidence came from.
- [B.1.4 - Contextual and Temporal Aggregation (B.1.4)](05-b14-b14-contextual-and-temporal-aggregation.md) — Helps teams aggregate ordered steps, phases, or time windows correctly when sequence or context changes the meaning of the result.
- [B.1.5 - Γ_method — Order‑Sensitive Method Composition & Work Enactment (B.1.5)](06-b15-b15-_method-ordersensitive-method-composition-work-enactment.md) — Helps teams compose multi-step procedures without mixing up the blueprint, the execution, and the resources consumed.
- [B.1.6 - Work-Resource Aggregation (B.1.6)](07-b16-b16-work-resource-aggregation.md) — Helps teams total up effort, cost, or materials across actual work performed without double-counting or reading plans as results.
- [B.2 - Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification (B.2)](08-b2-b2-meta-holon-transition-whole-reidentification.md) — Helps teams recognize when a group of parts has genuinely become a new, distinct whole that needs its own identity and responsibilities.
- [B.2.P - Emergence and MHT Precision Restoration (B.2.P)](09-b2p-b2p-emergence-and-mht-precision-restoration.md) — Helps teams avoid overclaiming "emergence" when what actually happened was an improvement, a metric jump, or a collection gaining a label.
- [B.2.2 - Meta-System Transition - System Specialization of MHT (B.2.2)](10-b22-b22-meta-system-transition-system-specialization-of-mht.md) — Helps teams handle the moment a coordinated group of systems becomes a new operating unit with its own objectives and responsibilities.
- [B.2.3 - Meta-Holon Transition With Episteme Result (B.2.3)](11-b23-b23-meta-holon-transition-with-episteme-result.md) — Helps teams recognize when a collection of publications or models has matured into a genuine, coherent body of knowledge.
- [B.2.4 - Capability and Functioning Whole Reidentification (B.2.4)](12-b24-b24-capability-and-functioning-whole-reidentification.md) — Helps teams decide whether a new capability signals a genuinely new whole or is just an improvement to the existing one.
- [B.2.5 - Supervisor-Subholon Feedback Relation (B.2.5)](13-b25-b25-supervisor-subholon-feedback-relation.md) — Helps teams make the oversight relationship explicit: who watches whom, what signal flows back, and what changes as a result.
- [B.3 - Trust and Assurance Calculus (F-G-R with Congruence) (B.3)](14-b3-b3-trust-and-assurance-calculus-f-g-r-with-congruence.md) — Helps teams assign and track confidence in a claim by naming the evidence, its scope, and the conditions under which trust should be revisited.
- [B.3.3 — Assurance Subtypes & Levels (B.3.3)](15-b33-b33-assurance-subtypes-levels.md) — Helps teams assess maturity of a claim by distinguishing conceptual alignment, logical correctness, and real-world validation as separate evidence types.
- [B.3.4 - Evidence Decay & Epistemic Debt (B.3.4)](16-b34-b34-evidence-decay-epistemic-debt.md) — Helps teams track when supporting evidence has gone stale and the stated confidence level no longer reflects current reality.
- [B.3.5 - Working-Model Relations & Grounding (CT2R-LOG) (B.3.5)](17-b35-b35-working-model-relations-grounding-ct2r-log.md) — Helps teams keep everyday structural labels readable while still linking each claim to a traceable, auditable justification.
- [B.4 - Canonical Evolution Loop (B.4)](18-b4-b4-canonical-evolution-loop.md) — Helps teams keep a system or knowledge body connected across design, operation, and improvement so that changes remain traceable.
- [B.4.1 - Observe -> Notice -> Stabilize -> Route (B.4.1)](19-b41-b41-observe-notice-stabilize-route.md) — Helps teams move from a raw observation to a well-formed question without prematurely committing to a single explanation.
- [B.5 - Canonical Reasoning Cycle (B.5)](20-b5-b5-canonical-reasoning-cycle.md) — Helps teams structure problem-solving by alternating between generating hypotheses, testing them logically, and checking them against evidence.
- [B.5.1 - Explore → Shape → Evidence → Operate (B.5.1)](21-b51-b51-explore-shape-evidence-operate.md) — Helps teams track where an idea or system stands in its journey from rough concept to reliable operational use.
- [B.5.2 - Abductive Loop (B.5.2)](22-b52-b52-abductive-loop.md) — Helps teams generate and compare candidate explanations for an anomaly or opportunity before committing to one hypothesis.
- [B.5.2.0 - U.AbductivePrompt (B.5.2.0)](23-b520-b520-uabductiveprompt.md) — Helps teams formally record a question or surprising observation so it can seed structured hypothesis work without pre-loading an answer.
- [B.5.2.1 - Creative Abduction with NQD (B.5.2.1)](24-b521-b521-creative-abduction-with-nqd.md) — Helps teams generate many distinct, testable hypotheses and surface the most promising ones instead of defaulting to familiar ideas.
- [B.5.3 - Domain-Concept Bridge (B.5.3)](25-b53-b53-domain-concept-bridge.md) — Helps teams keep their own vocabulary without accidentally importing hidden assumptions about what kind of thing a word names.
