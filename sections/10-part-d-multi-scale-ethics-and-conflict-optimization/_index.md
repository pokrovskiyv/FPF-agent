# Part D - Multi-scale Ethics and Conflict Optimization

- [D.1 - Ethical Value Plurality and FPF Boundary (D.1)](01-d1-d1-ethical-value-plurality-and-fpf-boundary.md) — Helps a team make the ethical weight of a claim visible so it can be inspected and owned rather than hidden in vague language.
- [D.2 - Multilevel Ethics For Holon Work (D.2)](02-d2-d2-multilevel-ethics-for-holon-work.md) — Helps a team spot when a change that benefits one group or level quietly harms another before acting on it.
- [D.3 - Interlevel Ethical Conflict Structure (D.3)](03-d3-d3-interlevel-ethical-conflict-structure.md) — Helps a team lay out who is in conflict, at which levels, and why, so the dispute can be examined instead of just argued.
- [D.4 - Ethical Mediation and Decision Use (D.4)](04-d4-d4-ethical-mediation-and-decision-use.md) — Helps a team turn a mapped ethical conflict into a concrete, bounded decision without pretending the conflict is fully resolved.
- [D.5 - Bias Audit and Ethical Assurance (D.5)](05-d5-d5-bias-audit-and-ethical-assurance.md) — Helps a team catch bias, fairness gaps, and hidden harm to people or subgroups that ordinary technical checks would miss.
