# Part A - Kernel Architecture Cluster

- [A.0 - Onboarding Glossary (NQD & E/E‑LOG) (A.0)](01-a0-a0-onboarding-glossary-nqd-eelog.md) — Gives teams a starter vocabulary for running open-ended search loops that return sets of options, not just a single "winner".
- [A.1 - Holon Ontic Foundation (U.Holon and Admitted Holon Kinds) (A.1)](02-a1-a1-holon-ontic-foundation-uholon-and-admitted-holon-kinds.md) — Helps teams decide what kind of thing is under discussion before making claims about parts, wholes, acting systems, or descriptions.
- [A.1.1 - U.BoundedContext Semantic Frame (A.1.1)](03-a11-a11-uboundedcontext-semantic-frame.md) — Prevents confusion when the same word means different things across teams, standards, or disciplines by naming the local frame in which each meaning applies.
- [A.2 - Role Taxonomy (A.2)](04-a2-a2-role-taxonomy.md) — Clarifies what a person, team, or system is being in a given context before attributing work, capability, or responsibility to them.
- [A.2.1 - U.RoleAssignment - Contextual Work-Role Assignment (A.2.1)](05-a21-a21-uroleassignment-contextual-work-role-assignment.md) — Records exactly who held which role, in which context, and during which window so that work attribution stays inspectable and replayable.
- [A.2.2 - U.Capability - System Ability Envelope and Measures (A.2.2)](06-a22-a22-ucapability-system-ability-envelope-and-measures.md) — Lets teams state what a system can actually do and within what limits, separate from who is assigned to a role or what work was performed.
- [A.2.3 - U.PromiseContent (Promise Content) (A.2.3)](07-a23-a23-upromisecontent-promise-content.md) — Separates what is promised to a customer or consumer from who is obligated and what work was done to deliver it.
- [A.2.4 - Episteme Evidence-Use and Status-Use Relations (A.2.4)](08-a24-a24-episteme-evidence-use-and-status-use-relations.md) — Stops reports, datasets, and standards from being treated as actors by making their role as evidence or status bearers explicit and bounded.
- [A.2.5 - RoleStateRelation@BoundedContext - Role State Space and Enactable-State Admission (A.2.5)](09-a25-a25-rolestaterelationboundedcontext-role-state-space-and-ena.md) — Lets teams check whether a person or system is currently in a state that actually permits them to perform a given piece of work.
- [A.2.6 - Unified Scope Mechanism (USM): Context Slices & Scopes (A.2.6)](10-a26-a26-unified-scope-mechanism-usm-context-slices-scopes.md) — Provides one shared mechanism for deciding where a claim holds, where a capability can deliver, and where a publication is valid.
- [A.2.7 - RoleRelationStructure@BoundedContext - Context-Local Role Relations and Representation-Lens Boundary (A.2.7)](11-a27-a27-rolerelationstructureboundedcontext-context-local-role-r.md) — Handles substitution, incompatibility, and bundling of roles inside a context without letting role labels silently carry capability or permission claims.
- [A.2.8 - U.Commitment (Deontic Commitment Object) (A.2.8)](12-a28-a28-ucommitment-deontic-commitment-object.md) — Makes accountability explicit by linking an accountable party to a specific obligation, scope, and time window rather than leaving it buried in a document.
- [A.2.9 — U.SpeechAct (Communicative Work Object) (A.2.9)](13-a29-a29-uspeechact-communicative-work-object.md) — Treats approvals, authorizations, and declarations as performed work with a named actor and timestamp, not just as text in a document or ticket.
- [A.3 - Transformer Constitution (Quartet) (A.3)](14-a3-a3-transformer-constitution-quartet.md) — Gives teams a single consistent way to say who acted, under which role, following which description, and what actually happened — without conflating planning with execution.
- [A.3.1 - U.Method: Context-Defined Way of Doing (A.3.1)](15-a31-a31-umethod-context-defined-way-of-doing.md) — Separates the idea of how something is done from any particular document, diagram, run log, or tool that describes or enacts it.
- [A.3.2 - U.MethodDescription: Description Episteme for a Way of Doing (A.3.2)](16-a32-a32-umethoddescription-description-episteme-for-a-way-of-doi.md) — Identifies what text, code, or diagram describes a method, keeping the description distinct from the method itself and from any work performed.
- [A.3.3 - U.Dynamics: State-Space and Transition-Law Episteme (A.3.3)](17-a33-a33-udynamics-state-space-and-transition-law-episteme.md) — Provides a reusable model of how something changes over time so predictions, observations, and gate decisions can be compared and audited.
- [A.3.4 - U.Transformation: Bounded Change Under Conditions (A.3.4)](18-a34-a34-utransformation-bounded-change-under-conditions.md) — Keeps track of what changed, from what starting condition, under which constraints, and who or what caused the change — all as distinct, checkable slots.
- [A.3.4.P - Transformation Ontic Precision Restoration (A.3.4.P)](19-a34p-a34p-transformation-ontic-precision-restoration.md) — Repairs vague terms like "pipeline", "flow", or "process" by identifying which specific kind of thing the word actually refers to in each context.
- [A.4 - Temporal Duality & Open‑Ended Evolution Principle (A.4)](20-a4-a4-temporal-duality-openended-evolution-principle.md) — Ensures teams track the difference between planned design and lived reality so discrepancies and updates are visible rather than silently overwritten.
- [A.5 - Open‑Ended Kernel & Extension Layering (A.5)](21-a5-a5-openended-kernel-extension-layering.md) — Keeps the core framework small and stable while allowing new domains and disciplines to add their own concepts without bloating or breaking the foundation.
